The Problem

During peak hours, university restaurants at Universidad de La Sabana experience long waiting lines. These lines can cause students, professors and staff to arrive late to classes, meetings and other activities.

Long waiting times can also create overcrowded walkways, slower table turnover and inefficient restaurant workflows.

Long Waiting Lines

Students and university staff can spend a significant amount of time waiting to order and pay for their food.

Overcrowded Areas

Lines can extend into hallways and walkways, making movement around the dining areas more difficult.

Workflow Bottlenecks

Some restaurant stations can become overwhelmed while others remain underused, reducing the efficiency of the dining service.

Our Solution

WebDining is a web application designed to make food ordering faster and easier at Universidad de La Sabana.

Users can browse menus, order meals, pay digitally and receive notifications when their orders are ready.

Digital Menu

Users can browse available meals, drinks and other products from participating university restaurants.

Digital Payment

Users can choose a digital payment method such as a bank transfer or debit or credit card.

Order Tracking

Users can follow their order from the moment it is received until it is ready for pickup.

QR Code Pickup

Users receive a QR code that can be scanned at the restaurant counter to verify and collect their order.

WebDining Features

For Clients

  • University ID login
  • Virtual restaurant menus
  • Digital payment options
  • Order status notifications
  • QR code order pickup

For Restaurant Staff

  • Receive notifications for new orders
  • Update order status
  • Verify orders using QR codes
  • Organize the restaurant workflow

For Administrators

  • Add menu items
  • Edit prices and descriptions
  • Remove unavailable products
  • Update menu photos

Target Users

Students

Students who want to order food quickly without spending time waiting in line.

University Staff and Professors

University employees and professors who need a convenient way to order meals during busy hours.

Restaurant Staff

Restaurant employees who need to manage incoming orders and update their status.

Restaurant Administrators

Administrators who manage menus, prices, descriptions and product availability.

Log In to WebDining

University users can access WebDining using their account credentials.
