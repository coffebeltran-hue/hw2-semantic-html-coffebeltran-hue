The Problem

During peak hours, university restaurants experience long waiting lines. These lines can cause students and staff to arrive late to classes, meetings and other activities.

Long waiting times can also create overcrowded walkways, slower table turnover and inefficient restaurant workflows.

Our Solution

WebDining is a web application designed to make food ordering faster and easier at Universidad de La Sabana.

Students, professors and university staff can browse menus, order meals, pay digitally and receive notifications when their orders are ready.

Digital Menu

Users can browse available meals, drinks and other products from participating university restaurants.

Digital Payment

Users can select a digital payment method and complete their order without waiting at the restaurant counter.

Order Tracking

Users can check their order status from the moment it is received until it is ready for pickup.

QR Code Pickup

Users receive a QR code that can be scanned at the restaurant counter to verify and collect their order.

WebDining Features

For Clients

  • University ID login
  • Virtual restaurant menus
  • Digital payment options
  • Order status notifications
  • QR code order pickup

For Restaurant Staff

  • Receive new order notifications
  • Update order status
  • Verify orders using QR codes
  • Organize the restaurant workflow

For Administrators

  • Add menu items
  • Edit prices and descriptions
  • Remove unavailable products
  • Update menu photos

Target Users

University Staff and Professors

Restaurant Staff
